# Workspace guide

This fictional guide is part of the bundled example collection.

## Choosing sources

Select which documents a question may use from the library. Asking is disabled while the selection is empty, and cached answers are kept separate for each selection.

## Reading the evidence

Every answer records the document name, page number and character offsets of the passage behind it. Opening a citation in the source reader highlights the exact cited text.

## Deleting a document

A deleted document leaves the searchable collection, its passages stop appearing in new queries, and later answers cannot cite them.
